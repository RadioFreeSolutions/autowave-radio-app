📻 About DriveWave

DriveWave delivers curated, high-bitrate live radio stations engineered for uninterrupted listening. Featuring quick mood filtering (Pop Hits, Latin & Spanish, Long Drive, Rock, Chillout), high-contrast Cockpit Driving Mode, and steering-wheel controls via standard MediaSession keys.

⚖️ Copyright & DMCA Disclaimer

DriveWave is a free audio directory and player interface. We do not broadcast, host, re-encode, or store any audio streams on our servers. All streams are direct, publicly accessible links provided by the respective radio broadcasters.
