📻 About AutoWave Radio

AutoWave Radio delivers curated, high-bitrate live radio streams engineered for uninterrupted listening. Featuring quick mood filtering (Pop Hits, Latin & Spanish, Long Drive, Rock, Chillout), high-contrast Cockpit Driving Mode, and steering-wheel controls via standard MediaSession keys.
