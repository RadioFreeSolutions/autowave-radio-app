1. Overview & Philosophy

AutoWave Radio ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed and published under RadioFreeSolutions, provides live internet radio streaming for drivers, car displays (Android Auto, Web Player), and everyday listeners.

We believe that listening to music should be private, fast, and completely free of tracking. Our fundamental design principle is zero data collection.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect ZERO personal data.

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use AutoWave Radio.
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not include Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, telemetry libraries, or third-party behavioral trackers.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: AutoWave Radio contains zero app ads and does not track Google Advertising ID (GAID) or IDFA.
  • No Location Data: AutoWave Radio does not request or access fine or coarse GPS device location permissions.

3. Local Device Storage

AutoWave Radio saves your favorite stations and recently played stations strictly locally on your device (using standard Android SharedPreferences or Web LocalStorage). This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

4. Network Permissions & Streaming Audio

To stream live radio, AutoWave Radio connects directly to publicly available shoutcast/icecast/HLS broadcast URLs provided by the respective radio stations. Your IP address may be visible to the station's streaming server as an ordinary web request, just like visiting any website in your browser. AutoWave Radio operates solely as an audio client and does not intermediate, proxy, or log your streaming traffic.

5. Broadcaster Copyright & DMCA Disclaimer

AutoWave Radio is a free audio directory and media player. We do not broadcast, re-encode, or host any audio streams on our servers. All audio streams, trademarks, station logos, and broadcasts remain the exclusive intellectual property of their respective broadcasters.

If you are a copyright holder or broadcaster representative and wish to update station metadata or request stream removal, contact us at: legal.drivewave@gmail.com. Legitimate requests are handled promptly.
