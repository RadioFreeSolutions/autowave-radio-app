Why AutoWave Radio is different

Live synced lyrics, on a live stream

Radio has no timeline, so lyrics are normally impossible to sync. AutoWave Radio matches the lyrics to the exact song on air, waits to witness the song actually start, and only then shows "Synced". Questions

Quick answers

The things people ask before they install.

Is AutoWave Radio really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no paywall, and no ads added by AutoWave Radio.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Your favorites and recently played stations are stored only on your device.

How do live lyrics work on radio?

AutoWave Radio reads the live song title, finds timed lyrics and checks that artist and title really match. It only shows "Synced" when it saw the song start while you were listening. If you tuned in mid-song, the lyrics show as "Not synced yet" and sync automatically at the next song, or you can tap the line you hear. You can also nudge the timing or search for any song manually.

Does it work with Android Auto and voice commands?

Yes. It connects over USB or wireless Android Auto, supports steering-wheel Next/Prev, and works with Gemini and Google Assistant voice commands.

Why is it an APK and not on an app store?

AutoWave Radio is currently distributed as a direct install. Android may ask you to allow installs from this source once. The Web Player needs no installation.

Will it drain my battery?

It is designed not to. A battery saver shuts down idle playback and releases resources, and idle streams are shut down automatically so a forgotten stream does not keep running.
